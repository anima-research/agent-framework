- A silent heartbeat tick that a gate rule batches, the framework's own or the heartbeat-mcpl server's, such as a debounce over `mcpl:push-event` that an operator sets or an agent adds with `wake_add_rule`, now wakes as the tick does when the gate lets it through at once. An agent whose share of the batch is silent ticks alone gets a silent heartbeat turn, prose suppressed, with the tick's separator. Before, it got an ordinary turn, whose prose was routed as speech. An agent whose share also holds other events gets one ordinary turn while they're unanswered, as a tick's wake merged with another's does, and its line leaves the tick out. Once the agent has answered them, their wake starts no turn, and the tick's silent turn runs. The tick itself wakes the agents a tick's wake does, not a conversation fork the gate also wakes; the batch's other events wake whom they did before.
  - `GateEventInfo`, which `EventGate.evaluate` takes, gains `silentHeartbeat` (`{ eventId, serverId }`), which marks an event as a silent tick. The framework sets it on its heartbeat's silent ticks, and on a push it has taken for the heartbeat-mcpl server's silent tick by that server's exact marker; nothing else sets it, and an event's `metadata`, a push's `origin` among it, doesn't. Before, the gate had no such mark, and a batched tick was a push like any other.
  - `WakeProvenance`, which the gate hands its `requestInference` callback, gains `silentHeartbeat`, carried by the wake each agent a batch's silent ticks reach gets, beside any wake for the batch's other events. That wake's reason and source are `heartbeat`. Before, every batch's wake had reason `gate:debounce` and source `gate`.
