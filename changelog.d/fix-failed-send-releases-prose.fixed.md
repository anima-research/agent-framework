- A round whose explicit sends all **failed** no longer silences its prose:
  the held prose is released to the locus and the turn's silence lifts.
  New opt-in `proseSilencing: 'round'` scopes a send's silencing to its own
  round, so an early send in a long tool-using turn no longer discards the
  turn's closing prose (default `'turn'` is unchanged).
