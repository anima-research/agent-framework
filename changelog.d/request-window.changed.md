- With `physicalWindowTokens` set, an agent's first request of a turn is kept within the model's window too, where before only the mid-turn projection guarded it, once a call had reported its prompt. Each compile that builds a request (a stream's start, a retry or restart, the direct `runInference` API, and a preview's dry run) now gives context-manager the request's system prompt and tools as `requestOverhead`, and the window as `requestLimitTokens`, so the compiled context, its injections, the system prompt, the tools and the reply the request asks for (the agent's `maxTokens`; a caller's budget that reserves less for the reply lowers the limit by the difference) come within it, as context-manager prices them. The compile is also given, as `requestOverhead.messages`, what the request may send besides the compiled context: the direct API's tool results ready before its compile, the tool-result guard's pending originals (the budget's room still keeps out the guard's reserve for them, at a quarter of their characters beyond their placeholders, and 1,600 an image, as without a window), a `prefillUserMessage`, and the turn a stream's start may end its request on. Outside that price, and reaching context-manager only through its calibration, are the request-only turns rendered again before earlier replies, which grow with the silent ticks the window keeps (69 tokens each by context-manager's estimator), another agent's rounds sent as transcript lines, a tool result that becomes ready during the direct API's compile, which only a framework-driven turn on the same agent can bring, and what membrane adds as it renders the request. Before, a budget near the window let a turn's first request pass it once the conversation filled the budget, and the provider refused it. A window the system prompt, the tools and those messages leave no room in now refuses the compile as over budget instead of sending the request. The turn ends with `errorType` `window_full`. It kicks no compression drain, doesn't unpin the turn's inputs or roll back a budget, and its `context-refusal` alert says what would make room. With a window and no `contextBudgetTokens`, the compile keeps the reply's whole `maxTokens` out of the window, at context-manager's default room of 96,000, where it used context-manager's default reserve of 4,000. Without `physicalWindowTokens`, the compile is given the overhead alone, which selects the same as before. Needs context-manager with `requestLimitTokens` and `RequestOverhead.messages`.
