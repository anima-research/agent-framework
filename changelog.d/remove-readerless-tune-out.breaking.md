- **Hosts that set `tuneOut.enabled`:** it's removed, and `create` refuses
  `tuneOut: { enabled: true }`. Residents are offered `tune_out` and `focus`
  only with a reader (`subconscious`, persistent or reader forks). A hold
  without one summarized nothing and woke no one, then handed the resident
  its backlog, undated, all at once when it ended. A tune-out still on record
  from such a hold ends at the next start, as one does wherever no reader is
  configured. A hold asked for after the reader has ended is refused, for
  `focus` (all-except) as for a single channel. The `tune_out` description
  drops its sentence about a silent hold, and the tool results their
  silent-hold notes. `tuneOut.announce` is unchanged.
