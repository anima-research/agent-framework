- **Hosts and judge authors:** for the reply judge's refusals and failed turns:
  - `EventGate.providerCallsReleased()`, which the framework calls where an operator releases a provider wait, where a surgery lets go of the store, and where an acceleration hold on an agent's calls ends, so the judge rules' batches held for a refusal are judged without waiting for the refusal's stated end, and those held for one that stated no end, or no time left, are judged then, not only once a later call gets a verdict. A turn that changes what the judge reads, or the host's quiesce, still holds them until it ends, and a hold that still stands refuses the call again. A host that constructs its own `EventGate`, with a judge that a hold of the host's can refuse, has that judge reject a refused call with an error whose `providerAdmission` is `'deferred'`, and whose `retryAfterMs` is the hold's time left when it states one, as the framework's own judge does, and calls `providerCallsReleased()` when that hold lifts. The gate counts any other rejection as a failed call.
  - `ReplyJudgeDeps.failedLastTurn` and its type `FailedLastTurn` (`at`, and `again`: `none`, `queued`, or `held` with an optional `until`), which the framework supplies so the judge can say a failed turn's wake is on its way, or isn't.
  - `UpcomingWake.when`, the time in words for a wake due at no time anyone can name, such as a turn held until an operator releases its provider wait; shown in place of `at`.
