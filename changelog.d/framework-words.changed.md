- `health--snapshot` lists each recent failed inference in the framework's words, from the inference log's new `errorWords`, such as "the model call failed (invalid_request, HTTP 400)". In 0.33.2 it gave the log's `error`, the failure's message cut to 400 characters, which a provider can fill with anything. An entry written before this release shows `null`.
- `gate_status` gives a failed reply judge's reason, as its rule's last verdict and in its note on a judge that keeps failing, in the framework's words, such as "the model call failed (server, HTTP 529)", or "the call failed" for an error the framework didn't write. The judge's own reasons, such as its timeout, read as before, except that an answer with no verdict reads "the judge's answer held no verdict", without the answer's stop reason or text, and a judge the gate times out reads as timed out even when it rejects as it's stopped. In 0.33.2 both gave the error's message. stderr still does, with the answer's stop reason and text. `ReplyJudge` rejects an answer with no verdict with those words as its message, and the stop reason and text on the error's `detail`.
- A script that a failed turn stops is given the failure in the framework's words as its reason, such as "the model call failed (invalid_request, HTTP 400)". In 0.33.2 it was given the failure's message.
