- **`materialize` no longer silently overwrites disk files changed by another writer** (#109). Before writing, each file's disk hash is compared against what the workspace layer last materialized; a mismatch means a shell/tool edit happened since, and the path is refused and listed in the result (`skipped`, with the remedy) instead of silently reverted — `force: true` remains the deliberate override, and refused paths stay pending rather than falling out of the incremental watermark. Files whose disk copy already matches the target bytes are left untouched (mtime preserved). The post-undo/redo `_config` restore keeps its overwrite semantics.
