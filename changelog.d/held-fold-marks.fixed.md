- A provider hold that keeps one of two equal requests now folds the other's marks into the one it keeps as the scheduler's merge does: the kept request takes a held turn's carried state (an explicit send it made, a silence it kept) when it has none, and stays re-armed only if both were. Before, a held turn's retry folded into an equal held wake lost that state, so the continued turn could post a text-only reply after a send it had already made, or speak after keeping silent; and the kept request stayed re-armed, or not, by which of the two was held first.
