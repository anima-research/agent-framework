- `InferenceRequest.answersMessageId`: the platform id of the one message a wake is for, in its `channelId`, as `getPendingRequests()` shows it. The framework sets it on a wake for one message from a channel or a push, the onboarding wake included, and carries it onto a rewind's retry and `/unstick`'s run. A refusal of the turn the wake opens is marked on that message, on a Discord channel, unless a rewind takes the refusal or the turn is an ephemeral run's. A wake the gate batches, even a batch of one message, and a module's wake carry none, nor does a re-armed turn that speaks in another channel than the message's. Wakes for different messages merged into one start answer neither.
