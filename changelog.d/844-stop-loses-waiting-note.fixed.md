- A reader's note waiting with a tune-out cancel isn't lost untold when the host stops before the hold ends: the stop keeps a notice of the loss in the store (`tune-out/<primary>/loss-notices`), and the next boot writes it into the persistent subconscious's context, with the reason and a turn to read it, the hold still in force and the cancel not run again. The stop traces it as `tune-out:deferred-cancel-dropped` (`stopped`), a reader fork's loss is dropped with a log line, and the subconscious's waiting `cancel_tuneout` answer says so. A notice written whose record the store wasn't brought in line with after (a stop, or a record write the store refused, between the two) isn't written again by the next boot: its row names the notice (`lossNoticeId`), read from the subconscious's stored rows as well as its window. While those rows can't be read, none of the notices the store's record keeps is written; they stay in the record until a retry reads them.
- `stop()` logs and traces (`tune-out:deferred-cancel-dropped`, `stopped`) every waiting cancel it drops, one with no note, or whose note was delivered already, among them, and tells no note lost for those; and a stop from a trace listener while the waiting cancels run leaves those not run yet to the stop, which tells them: none runs after it, and no retry is armed.
- A loss notice whose write meets a hold that begins at it (kept in memory, an empty id) is kept and tried again, the record keeping it until it lands, so a stop or a crash before the writes are stored doesn't lose it.
- A stop from a trace listener of a dropped cancel or of a loss notice's write keeps that loss, and the notices not written yet, in the store for the next boot, and a cancel asked for then, on the stopped coordinator, is told as the stop tells a waiting one. A loss told after the stop, of a cancel it found off its queue, is added to the store's record, and the stamp leaves its hold out where the store refuses that.
- A stop while the release that told a waiting cancel's lost note is kept in memory (writes held from that write on) tells the note lost, kept in the store for the next boot, since the stop loses that release.
- A loss told to an author from a listener of that author's loss notice write (its trace, or the trace of its failure) waits behind the notice in flight and is written next, so the author reads its losses in the order they came.
- A boot whose read of the store's loss-notice record throws arms a retry while the record is unread, which reads it first. Until it can be read, none of the persistent subconscious's notices is written, so the notices it keeps (a stop's, say) are written without waiting for an unrelated loss or the next stop, and in the order the losses came. Those read in later are checked against the subconscious's rows first, as a boot's are, so one written before the stop isn't written again.
