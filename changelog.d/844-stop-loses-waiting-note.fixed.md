- A reader's note waiting with a tune-out cancel is no longer lost untold when the host stops before the hold ends: the stop keeps a notice of the loss in the store (`tune-out/<primary>/loss-notices`), and the next boot writes it into the persistent subconscious's context, with the reason and a turn to read it, the hold still in force and the cancel not run again. The stop traces it as `tune-out:deferred-cancel-dropped` (`stopped`), a reader fork's loss is dropped with a log line, and the subconscious's waiting `cancel_tuneout` answer says so. A notice written whose record the store wasn't brought in line with after (a stop, or a record write the store refused, between the two) is no longer written again by the next boot: its row names the notice (`lossNoticeId`), read from the subconscious's stored rows as well as its window. While those rows can't be read, none of the notices the store's record keeps is written; they stay in the record until a retry reads them.
- `stop()` now logs and traces (`tune-out:deferred-cancel-dropped`, `stopped`) every waiting cancel it drops, one with no note, or whose note was delivered already, among them, and tells no note lost for those; and a stop from a trace listener while the waiting cancels run leaves those not run yet to the stop, which tells them: none runs after it, and no retry is armed.
- A loss notice whose write met a hold that began at it (kept in memory, an empty id) was counted as written and left the store's record, so a stop or a crash before the writes were stored lost it: it is now kept and tried again, the record keeping it until it lands (written twice where the kept write is stored too).
- A stop from a trace listener of a dropped cancel or of a loss notice's write now keeps that loss, and the notices not written yet, in the store for the next boot: the dropped cancel's notice was kept in memory only, an author's notices not written yet were left out of the store's record and written after the stop, and a cancel asked for then was queued on the stopped coordinator, which now tells it as the stop tells a waiting one. A loss told after the stop, of a cancel it found off its queue, is added to the store's record, and the stamp leaves its hold out where the store refuses that.
- A stop while the release that told a waiting cancel's lost note is kept in memory (writes held from that write on) now tells the note lost, kept in the store for the next boot: the note counted as told, and the stop, losing the release, told nothing.
- A loss told to an author from a listener of that author's loss notice write (its trace, or the trace of its failure) was written ahead of the notice in flight, which a failed write then put back behind it: the author read its losses out of order. It now waits behind the notice in flight and is written next.
- A boot whose read of the store's loss-notice record threw armed no retry, so where nothing else was waiting the notices it keeps (a stop's, say) weren't written until an unrelated loss or the next stop; and a loss told before the record was read in was written ahead of the older notices it keeps. The retry is now armed while the record is unread, and reads it first; until it can be read, none of the persistent subconscious's notices is written, so they come in the order the losses came. Those read in later are checked against the subconscious's rows first, as a boot's are, so one written before the stop isn't written again.
