- A refused turn's reaction now goes on the message the turn answered, in that message's channel, and the failure log's `locus` names that channel. Before, both took the latest message delivered from any channel: after a mention pushed from a channel the agent hadn't opened, the reaction went on an earlier message in another channel, or on none, and `locus` named that other channel or was `null`. A turn that answers no single message, such as a batch of wakes, a timer's or a subagent's, marks none, and its `locus` names the channel it was woken for, unless the wake gave only a channel id that no single registered channel answers to, as when two servers register it. A turn woken by a reaction or a deletion marks none either, nor does one woken by an edit, unless the coalescer delivered that edit as the agent's first view of its message.
- The retry a rewind queues now speaks in the channel of the message the shed turn answered, and its refusal is marked on that message. That covers the refusal auto-rewind (`refusalHandling.autoRewind`, off by default), `/unstick`, and the poison-history breaker, which is on by default. The retry doesn't see the shed message: in its place it sees the marker saying a turn was set aside. Before, the retry had no channel, so under the default `speech.locus: 'delivery'` what it said stayed in the agent's archive and never reached the person who had written that message.
- A push that delivers a new message, such as a mention in a channel the agent hasn't opened or a DM, now counts as the latest delivered inbound message, as a `channels/incoming` message does. The channel context's `incoming` names it, and under `speech.locus: 'latest-inbound'` it is the fallback locus. Before, no push counted. A channel-scoped coalesced push counts once its acceptance is acknowledged. Deletions, reactions and `chat:system` notices still don't count, nor do edits, unless the coalescer delivered the edit as the agent's first view of its message. Nor does a push that names no message, such as a voice report or a block of missed messages. A deferred push's message, delivered when assembly renders it, still doesn't count either.
- A coalesced message's acceptance no longer makes it the latest delivered inbound after a newer message was delivered. Before, for a `channels/incoming` message, a resend answered from its receipt after a failed acknowledgement, or a message delivered while the acceptance was being made durable, could leave the older message as the latest. A coalesced push, which now counts, is held to the same rule.
