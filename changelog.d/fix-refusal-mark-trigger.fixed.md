- A refused turn's reaction now goes on the message the turn answered, in that message's channel, and the failure log's `locus` names that channel. Before, both took the latest message delivered from any channel: after a mention pushed from a channel the agent hadn't opened, the reaction went on an earlier message in another channel, or on none, and `locus` named that other channel or was `null`. A turn that answers no single message marks none. That's one woken by several messages at once, whether they were merged while the agent was busy, quiesced or waiting out its provider; one the gate's batch woke, even a batch of one message; one a module's request woke; one woken by a timer; and a turn re-armed after its retries ran out, when its speech had moved from the channel that woke it. Its `locus` names the channel it was woken for, if it was woken for one, unless the wake gave only a channel id that no single registered channel answers to, as when two servers register it. An ephemeral run's refused turn still adds no reaction anywhere and its record names no `locus`, as 0.23.0 says, even when a channel message names the run in its `targetAgents`, and a rewind's retry of that turn carries no channel. A turn woken by a reaction or a deletion marks none either, nor does one woken by an edit, unless the coalescer delivered that edit as the agent's first view of its message. A refusal that the auto-rewind or `/unstick` rewinds is still not marked, as before, since the reaction is the last resort. The refusal they give up on is.
- The retry a rewind queues now speaks in the channel of the message the shed turn answered, as the agent's prose routing takes it, and a refusal of it that isn't rewound again is marked on that message. That covers the refusal auto-rewind (`refusalHandling.autoRewind`, off by default), `/unstick`, and the poison-history breaker, which is on by default. The rewind sheds the turn's newest exchange, which is that message when the refused round was the turn's first, and the retry sees the marker saying a turn was set aside in its place. Before, the retry had no channel. Under the default `speech.locus: 'delivery'`, a resident's retry spoke in the all-except hold's channel while one was open, and otherwise what it said stayed in the agent's archive, where the person who had written that message never saw it. A conversation fork's retry spoke in its home channel, as it still does. Locked, explicit and disabled prose routing route a retry's speech as they route any turn's.
- A push that delivers a new message, such as a mention in a channel the agent hasn't opened or a DM, now counts as the latest delivered inbound message, as a `channels/incoming` message does, unless it's ambient (tagged `chat:ambient`, where `chat:addressed` wins). The channel context's `incoming` names it, and under `speech.locus: 'latest-inbound'` it is the fallback locus. Before, no push counted. A channel-scoped coalesced push counts once its acceptance is acknowledged. An ambient push never counts, so chatter in a channel the agent hasn't opened doesn't move the fallback locus there. Deletions, reactions and `chat:system` notices still don't count, nor do edits, unless the coalescer delivered the edit as the agent's first view of its message. Nor does a push that names no message, such as a voice report or a block of missed messages. A deferred push's message, delivered when assembly renders it, still doesn't count either.
- A coalesced message's acceptance no longer makes it the latest delivered inbound after a newer message was delivered. Before, for a `channels/incoming` message, a resend answered from its receipt after a failed acknowledgement, or a message delivered while the acceptance was being made durable, could leave the older message as the latest. A coalesced push, which now counts, is held to the same rule.
