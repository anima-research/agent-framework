- Where a resident is told its plain speech was held as drafts, each draft is now named by its length in chars, counted as `drafts` reads and lists it, as well as its id, so the resident can tell which of its messages a draft is without the draft's words being repeated back to it. The held-draft notice and the turn-end receipt name a turn's drafts in the order the resident wrote them, for example "held as drafts d-a (11 chars) and d-b (28 chars), in the order you wrote them"; the next turn's catch-up of open drafts gives each one's length.
