- Wherever a resident is told that its words are held as a draft, or is given a summary of drafts it can still resend, each draft now has its length in chars beside its id, counted as `drafts` reads and lists it, so the resident has a measure of each draft without the draft's words being repeated back to it: the held-draft notice, the bounce notice, the notice for a message held after a refused `{{unsent}}`, the turn-end receipt's held and unconfirmed lines, and the next turn's catch-up for each draft it lists. The held-draft notice names one hold's drafts in the order the resident wrote them, for example "held as drafts d-a (11 chars) and d-b (28 chars), in the order you wrote them".
