- A persistent subconscious, or a resident that observes another
  (`observes`), whose window write-down failed at registration is sent other
  agents' rounds as its `foreignRounds` says (a transcript by default)
  once a branch move presents its window. Each branch move sets the mode
  again: the configured one where the agent's window is presented, `'raw'` where it
  stays the record.
- In a transcript, a call answers one result: another agent's result whose id a call of the watcher's had, and
  which comes after that call's own result (its own call compressed out of the request), is sent as a line
  ("a tool returned: …"), not as a second result of the watcher's call (#291).
- An agent derived from one that is sent other agents' rounds as a transcript (`deriveAgent`, shared or copy), and
  a conversation fork of such a template, are sent them so too (#291): their inheritance holds the rounds the parent's
  window held.
