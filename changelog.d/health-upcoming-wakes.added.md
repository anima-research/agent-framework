- `healthSnapshot()` reports each agent's `upcomingWakes`: what will wake it with no new event, soonest first and at most eight, as `{ at?, kind, certain }`. `kind` is the new `UpcomingWakeKind` (`heartbeat`, `reminder`, `self-wake`, `sleep-end`, `debounce`, `judge-batch`, `queued`, `provider-hold`), also set on `UpcomingWake`. No wake's description is included, since it can quote a reminder or a queue reason. The field is left out when the read fails, so an empty list always means nothing is due.
