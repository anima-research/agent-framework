- `history--search`'s hint, when a search stops at `maxScan` with no match, now says that nothing matched among the messages scanned and that this isn't a negative result for the range. With a `channelId`, it also says how many of the range's messages weren't searched. It used to say only that the oldest or newest N messages of the range were scanned, so a script reading `matches` alone could take a partial scan's empty result for the range's. Its advice on narrowing no longer names `author` after a stop at `maxScan`, since that filter applies only to the messages already scanned, nor `channelId` when the call gave one.
- `history--extract` with `author` or `excludeAuthor`, when its scan stops at `maxScan` with no message returned, now says in its hint that returning none isn't a negative result for the window.
