- **Two stderr lines for a headless host.** A deadline expiry can cut a call that the error policy then retries. The expiry can be a progress bound's cut, or a deadline that membrane reports. Before the wait, the cut now writes `[inference-timeout] agent=NAME stream cut after 150212ms; retry scheduled in 1000ms (2 of 2): REASON`. The reason stays on one line, and it holds the words of the resident's own failure note, not a provider's or an SDK's. The line says that a retry is scheduled, not that it was sent. A caller's stop during that wait writes `[turn] NAME: a stop ended the turn in its wait to retry; the retry wasn't sent: REASON`, and one during the tool-result guard's wait writes a line of the same kind. A caller's stop can cancel a live stream, through `abortInference()` or `Agent.cancelStream()`. Such a stop now writes `[turn] NAME: a stop cancelled the turn's stream after 12034ms; the turn ends there: REASON`. The caller's reason ends the line, in quotes when it is more than one plain word, and it isn't redacted. Traces, notes, the inference log, `failures.log` and the retry rules don't change. A host that prints these traces itself may show two messages for one event. connectome-host's piped mode is such a host.
