- **Two stderr lines for a headless host.** A deadline expiry can cut a call that the error policy then retries. The expiry can be a progress bound's cut, or a deadline that membrane reports. Before the wait, the cut now writes `[inference-timeout] agent=NAME retry scheduled in 1000ms (2 of 2): REASON`. The reason stays on one line. The line says that a retry is scheduled, not that it was sent. A caller's stop can cancel a live stream, through `abortInference()` or `Agent.cancelStream()`. Such a stop now writes `[turn] NAME: a stop cancelled the turn's stream after 12 s; the turn ends there (reason=REASON)`. The caller's reason prints as one escaped field, and it isn't redacted. Traces, notes, the inference log, `failures.log` and the retry rules don't change. A host that prints these traces itself may show two messages for one event. connectome-host's piped mode is such a host.
