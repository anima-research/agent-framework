- **Two stderr lines for a headless host.** A deadline expiry (a progress bound's cut, or a deadline membrane reports) whose call the error policy retries now writes `[inference-timeout] agent=NAME retry scheduled in 1000ms (2 of 2): REASON` before the wait, with the reason on one line. The line says that a retry is scheduled, not that it was sent. A caller's stop that cancels a live stream (`abortInference()`, `Agent.cancelStream()`) now writes `[turn] NAME: a stop cancelled the turn's stream after 12 s; the turn ends there (reason=REASON)`, with the caller's reason as one escaped field, not redacted. Traces, notes, the inference log, `failures.log` and the retry rules are unchanged. A host that prints these traces itself, as connectome-host's piped mode does, now shows each such event twice in its combined output: once in its own words, and once in the line's.
