- In XML tool mode, or with a membrane that gives a tool round no `roundContent`, a tool turn's speech reaches a channel only when its stream completes. When the stream instead fails, is stopped, is ended by a tool, is retried by the error policy or restarted for the context budget or by the tool-result guard, or is abandoned by a quiesce or `stop()`, a `[speech-not-posted]` notice now tells the agent that nothing it wrote before its tool calls in those rounds was posted, so it can send what should go out again. Before this change nothing said so, and a retry's request carried the words as already said. A turn its own `skip_reply` ends and a silent turn get no notice. With thinking on in XML tool mode, a round whose text was only thinking gets the notice too, since only the round's raw text is at hand there.
- A turn that fails, or that a caller stops, writes no `[delivered]` receipt, as before. When its rounds routed plain speech live (native tool mode, locus or hybrid routing), its `[inference-failed]` note, or the stop's `[turn-interrupted]` note, now adds that the speech was handed on for delivery as written and that no `[delivered]` line was written for it. In hybrid routing, when some of its speech was still being routed as the turn ended, the note also says it may yet be posted, and to check the channel before sending it again. Before this change those notes said nothing about speech already routed.
