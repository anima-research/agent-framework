- **A failed turn start's wait to retry no longer holds every other agent's turn and every queued event.** A turn's start can fail before its stream exists, at its compile or its stream setup, and the error policy can retry it. The policy's delay ran inside the scheduler's await. So no other agent's turn started, and no queued event was handled, until the retry had run. Now, when the scheduler begins a start that fails, its wait to retry runs beside the scheduler. When a stream's retry begins a start that fails, that wait stays inside the turn, as before. It holds no other agent. `runUntilIdle()` and `stop()` wait for a retry that is under way. A throw out of a retry that waits beside the scheduler now comes out at the scheduler's next pass, and not at the call that started the turn. It rejects `runUntilIdle()`, or the event loop writes it on its `Error in event loop:` line. If it comes while the framework stops, `stop()` writes it there, and after `stop()` has returned it is written at once. An agent removed during the wait gets one `inference:aborted`, with the reason "a retry waiting while the agent was replaced or removed is dropped before it sends anything". When `stop()` ends the wait, the reason is the stopping one. A compile that fails after its agent was replaced or removed reports no `inference:failed`, and the error policy isn't asked: the turn ends with one `inference:aborted` that says why. A turn that is dropped before it sends anything sends no such event if another agent has taken the name since the turn started. That agent may hold the name now, or it may have ended again.
