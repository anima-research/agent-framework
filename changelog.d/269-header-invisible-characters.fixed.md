- A source header no longer lets invisible or space-lookalike characters pass raw (#269). A value is quoted when it holds a format or default-ignorable character (zero-width characters, bidi overrides and isolates, fillers such as U+3164, the combining grapheme joiner, variation selectors), a space other than U+0020, or a `/` with whitespace on both sides; inside the quotes those characters are escaped as `\uXXXX`, so a quoted bidi override can't reorder what follows it. A label's leading header word (`thread`, `reply to`, `unscoped`) is recognized through such characters. Well-formed emoji sequences are exempt, joiners and selectors included, since a label is also a name a resident types back: `❤️ cats` stays as it is. Body text's header-shaped openings are marked through invisible characters too (`[​source:` with a zero-width space). discord-mcpl's `source-header.ts` (#63) renders the same header by the same rule.
