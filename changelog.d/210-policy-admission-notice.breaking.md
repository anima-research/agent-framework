- `onChannelAutoOpened` with `source: 'subscription-policy'` now reports a durable admission decision before transport reconciliation. Treat it as intent; await the registration call and inspect `getOpenChannels()` when confirmed live state is needed. `opened-by-delivery` reports a delivery that turned a closed channel open, once its transport is confirmed; a delivery that only reconfirms the transport of a channel already decided open or tuned out isn't reported. The built-in host notice now distinguishes selected-for-opening from currently open.
