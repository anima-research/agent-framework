- A context-maintenance pass no longer fails as a whole when one agent's readiness can't be read. context-manager 0.14, which agent-framework has required since 0.21.1, refuses its readiness queries while a strategy's initialization is pending (inside `switchBranch`, `fork`, `setStrategy` or `ContextManager.open`) or has failed, and a strategy can refuse on its own, as the autobiographical one does for a branch it hasn't reloaded. That agent's run now records the error, the agent gets its own `context-maintenance-failed` alert (`scope: 'agent'`), and the other agents run. Before, such a throw ended the pass for every agent, with one alert for the whole pass (`scope: 'pass'`). Anything else that throws while one agent's maintenance is prepared is that agent's failure in the same way. A failure that lasts, met by every pass, is recorded on each run, and logged and alerted once, then again when its text changes, every 15 minutes while it lasts (the alert webhook's cooldown for it, counted from the webhook's last post of it), or after that agent's maintenance has gone without a failure. `getContextMaintenanceSnapshot()` reports such an agent as `ready: false` with an `error`, and a strategy that can't give a progress snapshot reads as having none.
- While an operator surgery (`rollbackToMessage` or `suppressMessages`) or `switchBranch` holds the store, no maintenance pass starts and no over-budget drain kick is started, a running pass or drain kick starts no new tick, and the operator call waits for them to finish before it switches or forks the branch, so no maintenance work overlaps the move. Maintenance makes no provider call meanwhile: one made then, or one waiting at the agent's provider gate when the hold is taken, is withdrawn without being sent, which is no maintenance failure, and the next pass after the hold does that work again. The operator call never waits on a provider cooldown, or on the retry turn a cooldown queues, which the hold keeps from starting. `switchBranch` can therefore move the store after the call returns, and the API server's `branch.switch` announces the move it made once it has, while `branch.current`, the branch list, `/health` and `/status` read the store's branch whenever they report one. `maintenanceTick()` answers `ran: false` meanwhile, and `resume()` refuses with `OperatorActionError` (`agent-busy`), as the other operator calls do, including when the surgery took the store while `resume()` waited. Resume once the surgery has finished.
