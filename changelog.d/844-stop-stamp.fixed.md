- A reader's note waiting with a tune-out cancel is no longer lost untold when the host crashes, or stops while the store refuses the notice of its loss: each stop now stamps the holds it leaves on record whose waiting cancels it accounted for (`tune-out/<primary>/stop-stamp`), and a boot that finds a hold on record the stamp doesn't cover writes the persistent subconscious a notice that any cancel of it then waiting was lost, with any note left with it, and asks for its turn; the notice is kept and tried again while the store still refuses, traced as `tune-out:stop-unrecorded`, and the boot clears the stamp.
