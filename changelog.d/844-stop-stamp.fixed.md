- A reader's note waiting with a tune-out cancel is no longer lost untold when the host crashes, or stops while the store refuses the notice of its loss: each stop now stamps the holds it leaves on record whose waiting cancels it accounted for (`tune-out/<primary>/stop-stamp`), and each boot stamps its own run as covering none. For a hold on record the stamp doesn't cover, the boot reads that run's rows, the residents' and the subconscious's own, and tells the persistent subconscious what they show: that its note was delivered and the cancel's end not recorded; that the cancel it asked for, its own `cancel_tuneout` of that hold answered as waiting, was lost, quoting the first line of its note; or, where nothing shows, that a cancel it asked for then, if any, was lost with any note; a release that already told the note's loss gets nothing more. The notice asks for its turn, is kept and tried again while the store still refuses, and is traced as `tune-out:stop-unrecorded` (`found`: `delivered`, `asked` or `unknown`).
