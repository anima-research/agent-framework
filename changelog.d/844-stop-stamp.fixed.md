A store with no stamp at all, as one an earlier version wrote, counts as stopped cleanly. For a hold on record the stamp doesn't cover, the boot reads that run's rows, the residents' and the subconscious's own, and tells the persistent subconscious once what they show: that the cancel ran, its release stored and its end not recorded, with its note or saying the note didn't go out; that its note was delivered; that the cancel it asked for, its own `cancel_tuneout` of that hold answered as waiting, was lost, quoting the first line of its note; or, where nothing shows, that a cancel it asked for then, if any, was lost with any note. A release that already told the note's loss, or a loss the stop kept in the store, gets nothing more; a bare channel id two servers' holds answer to names neither, and the waiting answer now names the server (`serverId`); a later boot tells a hold again only with more to say, and asks the subconscious's turn once. The notice is kept and tried again while the store still refuses, and is traced as `tune-out:stop-unrecorded` (`found`: `ran`, `delivered`, `asked` or `unknown`).
