- An explicit send's result now says what it holds of the turn's plain speech. Where the turn's plain speech is routed (locus, hybrid or locked mode, in a turn that isn't private), a send that may have posted adds one line to its result. It names the place, and says the plain speech there is held as drafts, except in a round that keeps its speech private: on a turn routed as it streams, from the send's round on until a new message arrives or the turn ends, and on one routed at its end, such as the XML path, until the turn ends. A send whose place can't be told says the plain speech anywhere is held, and why. A send that posted nowhere, or one beside a `skip_reply` that ends the turn, adds no line, and a send a `code_execution` script makes adds none to the result its script reads. The line goes where the send's answer is read: after an error answer's text, as the whole of an answer with no data, and in no array that isn't content. So where a module's send adds the line, an answer that is a number or a boolean becomes text, that value then the line, and an object answer gains a `routing` field. A request whose history holds a send's result or receipt made after this carries its new words, the judge's requests over that history included, so its cache boundaries can fall elsewhere. A result or receipt stored before this keeps its words. The turn-end receipt's held line now gives each draft's reason after its id, for example "d-a (explicit send in its turn)".
