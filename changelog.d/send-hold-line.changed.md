- An explicit send's result now says what it holds of the turn's plain speech. Where the turn's plain speech is routed (locus, hybrid or locked mode, in a turn that isn't private), a send that may have posted adds one line to its result. It names the place, and says the plain speech there is held as drafts until a new message arrives or the turn ends: on the native path this round's included, or after this round where the round keeps its own prose (beside a same-round private think, a refused wake, or a `drafts` resend with no other send); on the XML path for the turn, earlier rounds included, or except a round where `skip_reply` was refused. A send whose place can't be told says the plain speech anywhere is held, and why. A send that posted nowhere, or one beside a `skip_reply` that ends the turn, adds no line, and a send a `code_execution` script makes adds none to the result its script reads. The line goes where the send's answer is read: after an error answer's text, as the whole of an answer with no data, and in no array that isn't content. The turn-end receipt's held line now gives each draft's reason after its id, for example "d-a (explicit send in its turn)".
