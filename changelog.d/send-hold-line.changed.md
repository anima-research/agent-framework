- An explicit send's result now says what it holds of the turn's plain speech. Where the turn's plain speech is routed (locus, hybrid or locked mode, in a turn that isn't private), a send that may have posted adds one line to its result. It names the place, and says the plain speech there is held as drafts: on the native path for the rest of the turn, this round's included, until a new message arrives; on the XML path for the whole turn, earlier rounds included. A send whose place can't be told says the plain speech anywhere is held, and why. A send that posted nowhere adds no line, and a send a `code_execution` script makes adds none to the result its script reads. The turn-end receipt's held line now gives each draft's reason after its id, for example "d-a (explicit send in its turn)".
