- An explicit send's result now says what it holds of the turn's plain speech. Where the turn's plain speech is routed (locus, hybrid or locked mode, in a turn that isn't private), a send that may have posted adds one line to its result. It names the place, and says the plain speech there isn't sent but kept as drafts the resident can resend. On a turn routed as it streams, that's from the send's round until a new message arrives or the turn ends, except speech a round keeps private, as one with `skip_reply` or a private think does. On one routed at its end, such as the XML path, it's the turn's speech, except speech a round keeps private, as one with `skip_reply` does, and none of it where a `skip_reply` ends the turn. A send whose place can't be told says the plain speech anywhere is held, and why. That's every send to a server whose send answers name no channel, such as slack-mcpl's `send_message`. A second send to the same place in one round, or a second whose place can't be told, adds no second line. A send that posted nowhere, or one beside a `skip_reply` that ends the turn, adds no line, and a send a `code_execution` script makes adds none to the result its script reads. The line goes where the send's answer is read: after an error answer's text or a text answer, as a paragraph tagged `[routing]`; as the whole of an answer with no data or an empty one; as a tagged text block after a content answer's own; and as one more element, a tagged string, after the records of an array that isn't content. So where a module's send adds the line, an answer that is a number or a boolean becomes text, that value followed by the tagged `[routing]` paragraph, as for a text answer, and an object answer gains a `routing` field first, which the answer's own `routing` field overrides. A request whose history holds a send's result or receipt made after this carries its new words, the judge's requests over that history included, so its cache boundaries can fall elsewhere. A result or receipt stored before this keeps its words. The turn-end receipt's held line now gives the drafts' reasons, grouped by reason, for example "d-a, d-b (explicit send in its turn)".
