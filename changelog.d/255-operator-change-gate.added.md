- **An operator-change gate for imposed body changes** (#255). With `FrameworkConfig.operatorChangeGate`, the framework's own operator mutations are resolved to an absolute change and handed to the host's gate, which stages it and answers with a receipt: accepted and queued, not applied. They are a `host/command` undo (by turns or by messages), hide or unstick, and an operator's or module's mutating `agent_settings` or tool-presentation call. The host applies a staged change only through `applyResolvedOperatorChange`, under a held safe-boundary lease. Without a gate, every operator path behaves as before.
  - Each cut and hide attempt is journaled in the store (`operator/changes`), and recovery settles it from that record; an unstick keeps its own journal (`operator/unstick`). `getOperatorChangeRecord` reads a change's record, `resolveOperatorChange` settles an attempt whose outcome can't be told (or `agent-framework-recover`, offline), and `restoreOperatorChangeSource` restores the source of a cut that didn't commit.
  - An unstick is a planned, journaled operation: it sheds the newest exchanges a step at a time until a re-run responds.
  - An applied undo-turns receipt reports what its cut removed (`messagesRemoved`), and its resolved change records how many messages followed the cut when it was staged (`messagesAfter`), as undo-messages already did.
  - `previewSurgeryMarks` returns the store and branch it previewed (`context: {storeId, branch}`), and `getStoreIdentity()` names the store, so a host can bind a staged surgery to where it was previewed.
  - A staged change's Discord awareness marks can be chosen at staging (`prepare({ id, staged: true, … })`), and are activated by the change once its body change is established.
- **`runAtSafeBoundary(opts, fn)`** holds a store reservation across a host callback, with no turn running, for work that must not interleave with one (#255).
- **A tool call carries who initiated it** through every redispatch: the agent itself, a puppet, the host or an operator. So a gate can tell an imposed change from the agent's own (#255).
- **`ModuleContext.addMessage` delivery options** (#255): a targeted, receipted delivery, optionally made durable before its receipt says so.
- **`Module.getSubconsciousTools()`** offers a module's tools on the subconscious's surface (#255).
