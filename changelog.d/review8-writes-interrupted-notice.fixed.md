- At boot, a result in the recipient's store counts as an interrupted job's own only when it was produced (`metadata.dendrite.producedAt`) at or after the job's record was created. A single-use name whose counter aged out of the registry starts again at incarnation 1, so a result an earlier job of that name and incarnation delivered doesn't stop the recipient from being told that the new job ended without a result. 0.32.0 didn't look in the store: it looked for the job's result among the registry's held mail by the job's name alone, so an earlier job's result of that name still held there stopped the recipient from being told, and a result already stored and released went unseen, the recipient told the job ended without a result.
- At boot, when the recipient's store can't be read for an interrupted job's result, the recipient isn't told that the job ended without a result. The job stays in the registry with its end unwritten, and the next boot reads the store again.
- At boot, an interrupted agent's unread notice passed on to the primary carries where it came from (`metadata.passedOn`: the agent's record and the row), so a later boot that finds the record still interrupted (the earlier boot's settle failed, or the host crashed before it) passes on none the primary's store already holds.
