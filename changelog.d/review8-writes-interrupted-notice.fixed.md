- At boot, a result in the recipient's store counts as an interrupted job's own only when it was produced (`metadata.dendrite.producedAt`) at or after the job's record was created. A single-use name whose counter aged out of the registry starts again at incarnation 1, and a result an earlier job of that name and incarnation delivered stopped the recipient from being told that the new job ended without a result.
- At boot, when the recipient's store can't be read for an interrupted job's result, the recipient isn't told that the job ended without a result. The job stays in the registry with its end unwritten, and the next boot reads the store again. Before, the failed read counted as no result, so the recipient could be told the job ended without a result when the result was in its store.
