- The `[inference-failed]` note after a turn with tool rounds no longer says that the model call "produced no response, so nothing was sent". It says that the turn failed (with the cause, when one is known); when any round reached the context with its results, "Its earlier rounds ran, and their tool calls ran as their results show."; and when the stream ended while its last round's tool calls were running, "Its last round's tool calls were started, but the stream ended before their results came back, so that round isn't in your context; those calls may have taken effect."
