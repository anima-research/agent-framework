- 0.28.0's entry for a subconscious whose `subconscious.model` names another
  model said that, with no physical-window projection, its `maxStreamTokens`
  restart is its only window guard. Where membrane knows the provider's cache
  convention, the count that restart compares leaves out cache reads and
  writes, so there, while most of a prompt is cached, nothing guards that
  subconscious's window mid-turn. The docs of `maxStreamTokens` and
  `physicalWindowTokens` now say what the count is.
