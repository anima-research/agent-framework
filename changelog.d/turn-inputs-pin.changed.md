- **A turn keeps the messages it opened with whole.** At a start that opens a turn, the framework pins raw, with context-manager's `pinRange` and the name `agent-framework:turn-inputs`, the rows of the agent's window (#291) that it sees after both the newest of its own messages there and its consumed watermark (where its window stood as of its last committing compile, or of its last tool boundary whose injected messages its stream carries), in the window's order, starting at the first such row that holds more than tool results. Those are the rows presented to it since the mark, a row stored before the mark and presented after it included, and no row stored and not yet presented, which isn't in its window; in legacy mode, where the window is the record, the rows stored since. A mark taken before the agent's window was written down (an agent registered where its window wasn't yet written down holds one until its first compile) is read the same way once the window is: so a row presented to the agent again after the write-down is among the turn's rows, and so are the rows it hadn't read before that row. Until the turn ends, or a compile drops the pin as the next bullet says, its compiles, a `maxStreamTokens` restart's included, render those rows raw, where 0.30.0 lets compression fold them once they leave the recent window, so a long turn whose older rounds are summarised no longer loses the request it is answering. Their tokens count against the budget. A row inside the range that the agent doesn't see, such as a tuned-out message, stays out of its view, as before.
- **When a committing compile refuses as over budget (`OverBudgetError`) while a pin of that name is held,** the pin is removed, a `[turn-inputs]` line is logged, and the compile runs once more, so the turn goes on as it would have without the pin, a refusal included. A dry run never removes it.
- **The pin is removed** when the turn ends, whether it settles or its start or continuation is dropped, at `stop()`, and otherwise at the agent's next start that opens a turn, which first removes any pin of that name, one left by a process that crashed included. A silent heartbeat tick's start removes one too, and pins nothing. While the turn holds the pin, `listPins()` on the agent's context manager shows it. An agent whose strategy has no pins gets none.
