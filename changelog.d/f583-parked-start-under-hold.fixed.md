- **Hosts with a `providerHold`:** a wake parked on provider admission behind
  an auxiliary call (compression's kind) is now held as a new wake is if a
  provider hold stands for its agent when the auxiliary work it waited for
  has settled, and it starts with the wakes the hold keeps once the hold
  releases. Before, it went on into its turn's call: when the auxiliary
  call's failure had armed the host's hold, that call went into the quota
  the host had just said was spent.
