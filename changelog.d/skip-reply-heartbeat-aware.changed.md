- `skip_reply` with `wake_in_seconds` names the heartbeat's wake when it comes first. When the heartbeat's next tick or a pending reminder is due before the self-wake, and the agent's rules let it wake the agent, the confirmation says so instead of promising the self-wake: `Turn ended; nothing sent. Your heartbeat (a check-in prompt, every 10 min) wakes you first, in ~300s, and that wake cancels your ~900s self-wake: set it again then if you still want it.` Any turn start cancels a pending self-wake, so the heartbeat's turn ends it. A skip with no wake, or a self-wake due first, reads as before. `Heartbeat.upcomingWakes()` now gives each wake's `kind` (`heartbeat` or `reminder`) and takes an optional `whose` (`"its"`, the default, as the reply judge is told, or `"your"`); the `HeartbeatUpcomingWake` type is exported.
