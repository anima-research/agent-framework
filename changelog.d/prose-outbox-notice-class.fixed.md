- Prose outbox: automatic notices (`routeSpeech(..., { notice: true })`, for framework-authored notices such as the failure notice in #178) are their own class. They are kept until delivered by default (`proseOutbox.noticeMaxAgeMs` to bound them) and give way to the size caps only after the agent's own entries. A failure notice is written at the start of an outage, so under the speech age limit it was the entry most likely to expire: the notices that reported drops were the ones dropped (the Librarian, 2026-09-26). The agent's notes about a queued notice now say "The automatic notice", not "Your reply", and no longer tell it the human missed its words.
